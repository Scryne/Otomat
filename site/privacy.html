Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

Who uses Otomat

Otomat is used by one person, its operator, to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. It has no sign-up, no other users and no public interface besides this website.

YouTube API Services

Otomat uses YouTube API Services. By using Otomat you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Otomat accesses, with the operator's authorization, only the operator's own channel: it uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions and caption tracks, and reads the channel's own aggregated analytics (such as views and average view percentage per video).

What data is stored

  • The IDs of videos Otomat uploaded and aggregated analytics numbers for those videos, kept in a private repository to choose future topics.
  • OAuth credentials for the operator's channel, kept as encrypted secrets and used only by Otomat.

Otomat does not collect, store or share any information about viewers or about any other YouTube user.

Revoking access and deletion

The authorization can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored video IDs and analytics numbers are deleted within 30 days after access is revoked or on request.
