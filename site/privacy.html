Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

Who uses Otomat

Otomat is used by one person, its operator, to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channels (Inkwell Oddities Stories, One Ticket Back and Lanternbug Tales). It has no sign-up, no other users and no public interface besides this website.

YouTube API Services

Otomat uses YouTube API Services. By using Otomat you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

With the operator's authorization, Otomat accesses only the operator's own channels. On those channels it:

  • uploads the operator's own long-form videos and their short clips, as private videos scheduled to become public, and sets their custom thumbnails (One Ticket Back);
  • sets each video's title, description, tags, category, audience (made for kids or not), privacy status, scheduled publish time, translated titles and descriptions, and caption tracks; it can also make a video private again;
  • updates the channel's own description, keywords and banner image;
  • reads the channel's own aggregated analytics (such as views, engaged views, watch time, average view percentage, audience retention and traffic sources per video) and the list of the channel's own uploads.

Buffer

Short videos for Inkwell Oddities Stories and Lanternbug Tales are uploaded through the operator's own account at Buffer, a social media publishing service that has its own YouTube authorization from the operator. Buffer receives the video file, its title and description. While Buffer fetches it, the video file is briefly hosted on this website and is removed right after. Buffer's handling of data is described in the Buffer Privacy Policy.

What data is stored

  • The IDs, titles and scheduled times of videos Otomat published and aggregated analytics numbers for those videos, kept in the operator's private repository to choose future stories.
  • OAuth credentials for the operator's channels, kept as encrypted secrets of that repository and on the operator's own computer, and used only by Otomat.

Otomat does not collect, store or share any information about viewers or about any other YouTube user.

Revoking access and deletion

The authorization can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. The operator deletes the stored video IDs and analytics numbers within 30 days after access is revoked or on request.
