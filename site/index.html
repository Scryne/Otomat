Otomat

Otomat is a private, single-user tool. It makes story videos from public sources and publishes them to YouTube channels owned by its operator: Inkwell Oddities Stories (short vertical videos of true, strange stories from history), One Ticket Back (animated history films of about ten minutes, with short vertical clips from each film) and Lanternbug Tales (Aesop's fables; paused).

What it does

  • Takes each story from a public source: Wikipedia's list of unusual articles or a public-domain edition of Aesop's fables. The source and its license are credited in every video description.
  • Writes the on-screen text or narration with an AI language model and checks every fact, name and number against the source text before a video is made.
  • Uses an illustration made with an AI image model, or a freely licensed image from Wikimedia Commons with its author and license credited. Narration, where used, is a synthetic voice; music is by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0).
  • For the films, draws scenes with AI image and video models from a sourced script narrated by a synthetic voice; every number and name in the narration is checked against the source. The film description lists its sources.
  • Publishes short videos through the operator's own Buffer account, then uses the YouTube Data API on the operator's own channels to set the title, description, tags, scheduled publish time, translated titles and descriptions and caption tracks. Films and their clips are uploaded with the YouTube Data API as private videos scheduled to become public, with a custom thumbnail.
  • Reads those channels' own YouTube Analytics to choose future stories and to compare films.

What it does not do

  • It has no other users and does not access any channel other than the operator's own.
  • It does not download, scrape or redistribute YouTube content.
  • It does not collect data about viewers.
