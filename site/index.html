Otomat

Otomat is a private, single-user tool. It turns open public datasets into short, vertical data-visualization videos and publishes them to one YouTube channel owned by its operator: Earth in Numbers.

What it does

  • Reads open data: the U.S. Geological Survey earthquake catalog, World Bank World Development Indicators and Eurostat. Every video credits its source and license on screen and in the description.
  • Renders a map or chart animation from that data, with synthetic narration and captions.
  • Uploads the video to the operator's own channel through the YouTube Data API and reads that channel's own YouTube Analytics to choose future topics.

What it does not do

  • It has no other users and does not access any channel other than the operator's own.
  • It does not download, scrape or redistribute YouTube content.
  • It does not collect data about viewers.
