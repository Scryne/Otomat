## Kanal açıklaması (EN)

One Ticket Back puts you inside history. Each film hands you a one-way ticket to a real moment in the past, and you
live it: what you would pay, what you would eat, what could kill you, and how it would really end.

Every fact comes from the sources listed under each video. Illustrations, animation and narration are made with AI
tools; every story is researched and checked line by line against its sources.

New films every week. Where should your next ticket take you? Tell us in the comments.
